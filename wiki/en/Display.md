# Display

Three pages: **FLIGHT**, **F3K**, **LAUNCHES**. Switch with the buttons at the bottom or by swiping.

## FLIGHT
![FLIGHT](../../docs/bilder-simulator/DLG%20-%20Sim%20800x480%2019%20Englisch%20Alarm.png)

- **Flight mode** band on top: name from Ethos, colour per mode. On a change the name is announced (Launch, Zoom, Speed, Thermal, Cruise, Landing, Normal; otherwise «Flight mode» and the number).
- **Flight time** of the running flight (otherwise the last one) and the launch number.
- **Launch height**: live during the launch, then the value of this launch; below the best of the list.
- **Vario**: number and bar (climb green, sink blue). Display only – no vario tone, no thermal hint.
- **Altitude** with the highest altitude of the flight, **RX battery** and **Link** with their lowest values.

## LAUNCHES
The last 50 launches (newest on top) with launch height and flight time, the best in green. **X** deletes one launch (with question; later numbers move up), **DELETE ALL** clears the list and the lowest values. Without touch: menu «Delete launch no.» / «Delete all launches».
